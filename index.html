What happens inside the machine

  1. Your tap water flows in

    A built-in filter takes out chlorine, bad taste and odour. The minerals stay in.

  2. The plates switch on

    Current runs between platinum-dipped titanium plates. The K8 has eight. Minerals drift towards the negative side.

  3. Hydrogen is born

    At the negative plate, hydrogen ions pick up electrons and become molecular hydrogen, H₂. The water around it turns alkaline.

  4. Two waters come out

    Alkaline, hydrogen-rich Kangen Water for your glass. Acidic water leaves through a separate hose, for cleaning.

Hydrogen doesn't keep. So it's made as you pour.

It's the smallest molecule there is, so it escapes from water over time. That is why bottled hydrogen water fades on the shelf, and why this machine sits at your tap.

The ORP meter

ORP shows whether a water tends to oxidise things, like rust on iron, or the opposite. Above zero is oxidising. Below zero is antioxidant. A hand-held meter reads it in seconds.

Typical readings: tap +200 to +400 mV, Kangen −400 mV or lower.

Frequently asked

What exactly is Kangen Water?

Kangen Water® is ionized alkaline water produced by Enagic's water ionizers through electrolysis. "Kangen" is Japanese for "return to origin". It is alkaline (pH 8.5–9.5), hydrogen-rich, and has a negative ORP.

How is it different from my RO purifier?

RO pushes water through a membrane, which strips out minerals along with contaminants and leaves it slightly acidic (pH 6–7). A Kangen machine filters the water and then ionizes it, so it keeps minerals like calcium and magnesium, turns alkaline, and carries dissolved molecular hydrogen, which gives it a negative ORP.

Can't I just buy bottled hydrogen water?

Hydrogen is the smallest molecule there is, so it escapes easily: from plastic bottles while they sit on the shelf, and from any glass soon after it's poured. A Kangen machine makes it fresh at your tap, right when you pour, with no bottles to keep buying.

Is it safe to drink every day?

Enagic intends Kangen Water at pH 8.5 to 9.5 for daily drinking and cooking. If you're new to alkaline water, start at pH 8.5 and increase gradually. Strong Kangen, Beauty and Strong Acidic water are not for drinking.

Which machine is right for me?

For most families the LeveLuk K8 (flagship) or SD501DX are ideal. Singles and couples often start with the JRIV.
