The Science, Explained

Three things you can measure — not just claims

Kangen Water is defined by three properties, and each one can be shown in a simple test you can watch for yourself.

Property 01 · Anti-oxidation

Water that resists oxidation

Oxidation is what browns a cut apple and rusts metal. A water's Oxidation-Reduction Potential (ORP), measured in millivolts, shows how oxidizing or reducing it is.

Positive ORP (soda and tap: +200 to +400 mV) is oxidizing. Negative ORP is antioxidant — and Kangen Water measures around −400 mV or lower.

Property 02 · Alkalization

See the pH change with your own eyes

A drop of pH indicator reveals a liquid's acidity instantly. Sodas and sports drinks turn red (around pH 3). Tap and bottled water sit near neutral. Kangen drinking water turns deep purple.

Kangen drinking water is alkaline at pH 8.5–9.5 and keeps naturally occurring minerals in the water.
Property 03 · Micro-clustering

Broken into smaller clusters

Enagic describes ionization as breaking water's molecular clusters into smaller groupings — a concept known as “micro-clustering,” and the idea behind the well-known tea-bag test.

The tea-bag test: a bag dropped in cold Kangen Water appears to brew faster than in cold regular water — a simple test often used to illustrate the idea.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked

Kangen Water® is ionized alkaline water produced by Enagic's water ionizers through electrolysis. "Kangen" is Japanese for "return to origin" — water restored to a clean, mineral-rich, antioxidant state. It is alkaline (pH 8.5–9.5), hydrogen-rich, and has a negative ORP.

RO water is acidic (pH 6–7) and strips out all minerals along with contaminants. Kangen Water retains beneficial minerals like calcium and magnesium, is alkaline, and carries antioxidant potential (negative ORP) that RO water simply cannot provide.
