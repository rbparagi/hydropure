        The questions worth asking

        Is this multi-level marketing?

        It works on several levels, so yes, in that sense. Commission is paid when a machine is sold. Signing someone up, with no machine sold, should pay nothing.

        Can I lose money?

        Yes. If you buy a machine mainly for the business and don't sell enough, you won't recover its price. Travel, events and tax come out of your earnings too.

        Do I get all eight points on my own sale?

        No. Your own sale pays from one to six points, depending on the line it opens. The rest goes to the people above you.

        What does "group sales" mean?

        Your own sales plus all the sales in your lines. Group sales unlock the higher lines: .

        Can my rank drop, or do I pay to renew?

        The 2026 plan says ranks don't go down and there is no annual renewal. Check both in the distributor agreement. Also ask whether you must keep making your own sales to stay eligible.

        How long does a machine last?

        Some presentations divide the price over 25 years. HydroPure lists a five-year warranty on the SD501 DX, so plan on that and treat anything longer as a bonus. Filters are extra.

        Should I promise people better health?

        No. It's water. Let people taste it, see the tests and decide. Don't sell a machine as a treatment for anything.
